The sticky estimate

For every ε > 0, sufficiently dense shadings of a nonempty family of δ-tubes satisfying the Convex Wolff Axioms at every scale have union volume at least δε, for all sufficiently small δ.

Wang–Zahl, Theorem 5.2. The blueprint states the losses and quantifiers precisely, follows the proof through its projection and grain estimates, and records differences between paper statements and the formalization.
