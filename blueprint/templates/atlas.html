Scope, sources and how to read the two kinds of connection

The endpoint is the finite sticky estimate, WZ2 Theorem 5.2. The expert atlas supplies the branch structure, mathematical explanations and proposed connections. Its uncompiled Lean sketches have been replaced by links to audited declarations.

Solid arrows have a nonempty path of references in the compiled sticky proof. Types, definitions and structural references count as dependencies too: a used proposition is not thereby proved. Intermediate declarations may be folded into an arrow; click it to inspect every step.

Dashed arrows retain an expert connection without such a path between the selected versions in this root's closure. A missing match, an unused version and two cards sharing one declaration have different explanations in the edge audit. A dashed arrow does not assert that the mathematical argument is false.

Each card presents the reviewed manuscript formulation, its correspondence Remark and the actual Lean versions. The original expert formulation remains available for comparison. Branch colours do not indicate completion. The External view opens the implemented proofs inside labelled frames; Mathlib remains a library boundary.
