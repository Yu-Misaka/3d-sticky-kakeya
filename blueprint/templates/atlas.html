Scope, sources and how to read the connections

The endpoint is the finite sticky estimate, WZ2 Theorem 5.2. The expert atlas supplies the branch structure, mathematical explanations and proposed connections. Its uncompiled Lean sketches have been replaced by links to audited declarations.

Solid arrows have a nonempty path of references in the compiled sticky proof. Types, definitions and structural references count as dependencies too: a used proposition is not thereby proved. Intermediate declarations may be folded into an arrow; open it to inspect every step.

Each solid arrow is also compared with the roadmap. It agrees when the roadmap connects the two nodes, directly or through other nodes; it is Lean only when the roadmap has no such path; it is a Lean detail when one end is a Lean version that the roadmap does not show.

Dashed and dotted arrows are roadmap connections without a supporting path. A dashed arrow means both Lean versions are used and the proof takes another route. A dotted arrow cannot be compared: a node has no Lean match, its version is outside the root's closure, or two cards share one declaration. Neither asserts that the mathematical argument is false.

Click a node to read its card beside the graph. Branch colours do not indicate completion. The External view opens the implemented proofs inside labelled frames; Mathlib remains a library boundary.
