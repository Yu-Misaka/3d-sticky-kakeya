How the dependencies are computed

References in declaration types, proof bodies, private helpers and inductive data are followed. Unlinked helpers are folded into edges and redundant edges are removed for readability. The proof atlas shows every reached blueprint-linked declaration in its whole-proof graph with every frame open, and shows the witness of each arrow when you select it. Lean and Mathlib are library boundaries; explicitly linked Mathlib declarations are terminal nodes.

These are references in the stored proof term, not a claim of minimal logical necessity. Type annotations, unused let bindings and arguments passed to a theorem still count. A reached theorem does not by itself establish equivalence to every informal formulation.
