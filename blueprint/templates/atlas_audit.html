What the audit establishes

Root: {{ atlas.root }}. Each solid arrow below has a nonempty kernel-reference path. The expert's graph never participates in the reachability computation.

Reachability is checked in the full projected declaration graph, including paths through other displayed nodes. Failure to find a direct displayed edge is not enough to declare a connection unsupported. When a selected declaration is outside the root closure, the comparison records that fact rather than asserting that its own independent proof has no such dependency.

References in types, proof or definition bodies, private helpers, projections, constructors and recursors are distinguished in the paths. A constructor or a proposition-valued definition is not classified as an independent proof. References retained in a proof term are counted even if a later simplification could eliminate them.

Shared declarations are explicitly identified: two mathematical cards using the same Lean declaration do not create a self-dependency. Correspondence Remarks, rather than graph reachability, delimit the mathematical scope of each match.
