How the comparison is made

Every solid arrow has a nonempty path of kernel references in the compiled sticky proof, and the expert's graph never takes part in that reachability computation. Reachability is checked in the full projected declaration graph, including paths through other displayed nodes, so a missing direct arrow is not enough to call a connection unsupported. When a selected declaration lies outside the root's closure, the comparison records that fact rather than asserting that its own proof has no such dependency.

References in types, proof or definition bodies, private helpers, projections, constructors and recursors are distinguished in the witnesses. A constructor or a proposition-valued definition is not classified as an independent proof, and references kept in a proof term count even if a later simplification could remove them.

Two cards that share one Lean declaration do not create a self-dependency. Correspondence remarks, rather than graph reachability, delimit the mathematical scope of each match.
